# 🤝 Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Avatar Shield to be a welcoming project for anyone trying to protect their
community from scammers. We pledge to make participation a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good faith; critique code and ideas, not people.
- Keep discussions focused and useful.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Using this project (or its issues/discussions) to promote scams, or to help
  anyone evade impersonation detection.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues, and contributions that
violate this Code of Conduct, and may block repeat or serious offenders.

To report a concern, contact the maintainers privately — via a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/tradernetwork/avatar-shield/security/advisories/new)
if it's sensitive, or by reaching the [Trader Network](https://github.com/tradernetwork)
maintainers. Reports will be handled discreetly.
